    raw_score (RF=1.0)     1.3206649447461245
    official total_score   0.9245183669982832
    cost-weighted RF       0.7000400598775689     <- the floor is 0.70
    n_feasible             100/100
    total_runtime          52.07 s  (mean 0.521 s/case)

**We sit essentially exactly ON the runtime floor**, 0.0057% above it. Alpha had
estimated 0.70802; Beta measures us deeper in.

🔑 **`max(0.7, RF^0.3)` has derivative ZERO in the floor region.** So a small
runtime increase costs nothing — not "approximately nothing", exactly nothing.

⚠️ **What this does NOT establish: how much headroom there is.** The aggregate
cannot distinguish "every case sits far below the threshold" from "every case
sits exactly at it" — both report an adjustment of 0.70. The 3.28x figure that
follows from `t/M <= 0.7^(1/0.3) = 0.3046` is only valid under the assumption
that all cases share one ratio, which is not established. **Small increases are
free; large ones are unknown.**

### What the top-5 table will add

It carries the same five columns. For each team, `total_score / raw_score` is
**their** cost-weighted RF:

* **RF_j ~= 0.70** → they are floored too → the field is fast → the median is set
  by slower submissions → our headroom is large;
* **RF_j > 0.70** → their runtime exceeds 30.5% of the median → a direct lower
  bound on the median, and a direct measurement of what being slow costs.

`raw_score` also gives the first view of **pure quality standings** with runtime
divided out, and `total_runtime` places our 52.07 s in the field.
