## 6. Method notes that cost time

* **Never ratio two differences.** `dt_LP`'s per-n ratio is `(C−A)/(D−B)`, two
  ~1 s numbers subtracted; four block counts came out with a ≤ 0 denominator and
  the ratio hit **1.9 × 10⁴**. The first scoring pass put the *shipped* gate at
  **−59 %** because of it. Band level or nothing.
* **A band constant deserves a band-level estimate.** REFINE changes every case
  in its band identically, so a per-n ratio only injects this box's ~17 %
  per-case noise into a threshold test that had 0.12 pp of rank margin. The
  control band's own dispersion (p10 0.959, p90 1.122) *is* that noise.
* **Chain of custody, checked not assumed**: `_l181_cur.json` (Aug-24, the pool
  times every projection rests on) is **100/100 identical in cost** to
  `results_L230_B1.json` (today's tree with the heavy kill switch on). Without
  that the ratio-chaining would have been an assumption.
* **`nohup sh -c '... > file'` does not survive**; `nohup sh script.sh > file`
  does. A 100-case A/B died after printing its header and looked like a pass.
* **Check the key name before trusting a gate**: the in-set results use
  `is_feasible`, not `feasible`. The first ship attempt reverted a *correct*
  tree on a `KeyError`.
