# L131 — the shipped submission loses score to the same ULP

L130 item 1. The submission was **not touched**: nothing here writes into
`build_submission/`, rebuilds the binary, or changes any md5. Everything below is
a read-only audit of `results_L114_48c_lp_anchor.json` plus an in-memory snap
re-scored by the official evaluator.

**Result: +0.0758%, officially measured, on two cases, with zero new
infeasibility.** Above the portfolio bar (0.05%), a quarter of the ship bar
(OOS NET 0.30%).

## 1. The shipped result has the L130 bug in it

`l131_abut_audit.py` counts each cluster group's `unary_union` components, then
recounts with the polygons buffered by 1e-9. A split that disappears under that
buffer was never a real gap.

| case | n | vgrp | spurious | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 69 | 90 | 1 | **1** | 2.842e-14 |
| 78 | 99 | 1 | **1** | 1.421e-14 |

**2 of the shipped result's 16 grouping violations (12.5%) are floating point**,
both on heavy cases. The audit's own baseline reproduces `1.236791670`, the
handoff's shipped 48c score to every digit, so this is the real anchor.

Two sites in `constructive_l124.cpp` have the defect:

* `:1039` and `:1069` emit `rx = x + it.offs[k].first`, with `offs` built by
  accumulation at `:450` — the exact `origin + offset` shape;
* `final_group_boundary_nudge` (`:1146`) rigidly translates a cluster by
  `out[b].x + dx`, and a uniform translation does not preserve `a.x + a.w == b.x`
  in doubles.

The file's own header already knew (`:28`): *"clusters have no slack and FP
translation breaks exact abutments"*. It was recorded as a reason to reject a
different feature, not as a bug in the paths that survived.

## 2. The fix does not need a C++ rebuild, and it is safe by five orders of magnitude

`check_overlap` (`iccad2026_evaluate.py:223`) ignores overlaps below **1e-6** on
both axes — *"touching edges OK"*. The gaps are ~1e-14 and a snap moves a block
by at most 1e-9. So closing them cannot manufacture an overlap violation, and the
fix can be a post-process on the final positions rather than a change to the
placer. `op_wrapper.py` is generated by `make_submission.build_op_wrapper_text()`,
so the change surface is one reviewable Python function and the binary's md5 is
untouched.

Constraints respected by the snap: preplaced blocks are never moved (position is
HARD), widths and heights are never touched (dimensions and area are HARD).

## 4. 🚨 The estimate was 3× too high, and the reason generalises

The first version of `l131_abut_audit.py` priced this itself, as
`cost * exp(BETA * dV_rel)`, and reported **+0.2196%** — against the official
**+0.0758%**.

It read `N_soft` from the results json. **An official results json has no
`nsoft` field** — that key is an L129 addition to its own output. The fallback
was `max(..., 1.0) = 1.0`, so `vr - spurious/N_soft` subtracted a full 1.0 of
relative violation and clamped to zero, removing *every* violation on those cases
instead of one.

🔑 **A per-case normaliser that silently defaults to 1.0 does not produce an
obviously broken number — it produces a plausible one, 3× out.** The audit no
longer prices anything; it counts, and points at the verifier. This belongs on
the 08-15 §3 list.

## 5. What is and is not established

**Established:** the shipped 48c result contains 2 spurious grouping violations;
removing them is worth +0.0758% by the official evaluator; the fix is a
positions post-process that cannot create an overlap and needs no rebuild.

**Not established:** what this is worth OOS. It is 2 cases out of 100 (12.5% of
that set's grouping violations), and the in-set magnitude depends on which cases
happen to carry an abutting cluster whose coordinates land on the wrong side of
an ULP. The mechanism is deterministic and applies to every case with an abutting
cluster, so the RATE should transfer; the per-set magnitude need not.

**Not done, by decision:** the submission is uploaded and frozen and stays that
way. This report is what a fix would be worth, measured rather than guessed, so
the decision can be made on a number.
