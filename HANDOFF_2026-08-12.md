## 2. What happened: the shape axis is closed

Full report `L122_REPORT.md` (this tree). Probes, per-case dumps and the
modified `l100_lp_speed.py` are on `m71-mechanism-screen` at `56bb2f8`.

**Route Ⓒ was picked, per 08-11 §3, and is RED — but not for the reason the
route was designed around.** Pinning closed-form shapes into the LP is
infeasible on 5/5 pilot cases, and *stays* infeasible at a **0.05%** shape
change, four orders below the trust region it was trying to escape. An elastic
phase-1 on the same matrix blames **separation** rows (5–25 of them), not area
and not bbox — a 100% bbox slack leaves the separations still blaming. The
mechanism is that a block wedged between two members of the same rigid unit has
a hard width ceiling (`dw_b + dw_m1 <= gap1 + gap2`) that the fixed topology
cannot relax. **Pinning is the wrong shape of answer; the LP has to stay free to
back off where the topology says no.**

That also explains the recorded "cheap LR keeps 0/100": at the linearised
optimum, stationarity on an interior shape column reads `g_w*w == g_h*h`, which
**is** the closed form's own KKT condition. Feeding the linearised LP's duals
into the closed form returns the shape it already has. The gradient must come
from a shape-**pinned** solve.

**L122, which came out of that diagnosis, works on quality and fails on price.**
Tangent cuts of the convex side of the area constraint, the non-convex upper
side dropped to `hard_ok` verification, plus a price on each block's own area.
100 cases, official evaluator, `dep_case`'s guard verbatim, min-of-3 timing:

| arm | weighted quality | gain | tLP |
|---|---|---|---|
| k=1 (shipped) | 1.236783247 | +2.3559% | ×1.00 |
| k=12 | 1.197354156 | +5.4688% | ×8.60 |
| **tangent R=1.5** | **1.202912100** | **+5.0300%** | **×2.33** |

99/100 kept, 0 regressions, 0 blocks over the 1% area limit. It **strictly
dominates k=3 through k=12** and captures **92% of the depth-12 gain at 27% of
the depth-12 time**. Control arm (band + price, no cuts) is worth +0.11pp, so
the gain is the cuts.

**And grid worst is RED at every range, monotonically:** −0.261pp (R=1.1),
−0.275 (1.2), −0.793 (1.3), −2.064 (1.5), against the shipped LP. Every arm wins
at s=1 — R=1.5 nearly doubles the shipped gain there — and every arm loses at
s≥2. **The frontier does not cross.**

### 🔑 The conclusion that outlives the mechanism

L122 is a **4.4× better time-for-quality trade than depth** and still does not
clear the bar. So the shape axis is not priced out because the mechanism was
inefficient — **there is no room to spend LP time at all.** Anything future here
has to be essentially **free (≈1.0×)**, not merely cheaper than depth.

That closes, together: L120's whole efficiency premise, and 08-11 §3's
alternatives **Ⓐ** (relax HPWL into the multipliers) and **Ⓑ** (min-cost flow).
Neither was implemented and neither should be: both are *more* expensive than
L122, and L122's problem is cost, not quality. Do not re-open either without a
mechanism that is free.

## 3. 🚨 Correction to the record

`HANDOFF_2026-08-11.md` §2 and `[[l120-lr-solver-in-flight]]` both said the area
linearisation "caps the trust region at rho=0.06; rho=0.10 already keeps 0/100
passes", which reads as a geometric or quality cause. It is arithmetic:

The band rows carry `slack = rho^2 * p`, so the band on the **linearised** area
is `[A(1-TOL)+slack, A(1+TOL)-slack]`, which is **empty** once
`rho^2 >= AREA_TOL = 0.008`, i.e. **rho >= 0.0894**. At rho=0.12 the LP returns
status 2 on every case. "0/100 passes" was an infeasible program, not a
rejected geometry.

rho *is* genuinely binding, though: **44.7%** of shape columns sit exactly on
the trust-region bound, and widening 0.06 → 0.088 moves the LP objective by
+0.13% to +0.71% per case. The cap was real and worth money — it just was not
where the record said it was.

## 4. Things that will silently mislead you (additions to 08-11 §4)

08-11's ten notes all still hold. Three more:

11. **Single-shot LP timing read ×1.95 where min-of-3 reads ×2.33.** That is a
    0.38× error on the exact number the ship/no-ship decision turns on. L100's
    rule (≥3 reps, take the median/min) is not optional for anything priced.
12. **The pricing bar moves between runs.** The shipped arm's own grid worst
    read +1.992% and +2.248% in two runs, purely from tLP noise on that arm.
    **Only within-run deltas mean anything** — always price the arm and its
    control in the same run.
13. **`build_and_solve(..., prune_B=...)` called directly is NOT exact.** The
    HPWL pruning is only exact because `solve_pruned` re-checks the dropped
    terms' signs and re-solves the offenders. Calling the builder directly
    leaves a lower-bound program whose optimum need not be the real one. The
    first cut of L122 did this and every number had to be re-measured.
