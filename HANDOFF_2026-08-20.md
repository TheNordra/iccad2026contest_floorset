## 4. 🚨 FOUR SILENT-FAILURE MODES HIT THIS SESSION (all caught, all recorded)

1. **Light-band screening.** `m77._specs()` sorts by n ASCENDING and scoring
   weights by `exp(n/12)`: `specs[0:16]` is **0.0126%** of the weighted score,
   `specs[0:128]` is 2.74%, and `specs[192:240]` carries 69.81%. A real mechanism
   was declared dead on the first slice.
2. **Empty antecedent.** L152's in-set run read 0/100 moved, +0.0000% — because
   the in-set 100 has **1** opposite-edge-pair cluster against OOS's **23**.
   Census the antecedent on the corpus you will measure on, first.
3. **Binary override ignored.** `m77_oos_probe` strips every `ICCAD_*` at import,
   so `oc._BIN` binds to the shipping exe before any restore runs and
   `ICCAD_CONSTRUCTIVE_BIN` is silently dropped on the normal path. Fixed by
   `l140_oos_soft_audit.py --bin`, which sets `oc._BIN` directly and prints it.
4. **Mismatched measurement bases.** Comparing a min-of-3 arm against a
   single-shot arm (bias alone manufactures a result), and applying a band-gated
   delta to all 100 cases (90 cases of pure noise manufactured a +0.13pp
   "saving"). Timing noise on this box: control's own spread p50 2.8%, max 8.9%.
