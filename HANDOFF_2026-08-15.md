### L125 — the beam (`L125_REPORT.md`)

Quality is the best any mechanism has shown on the twin screen: s2 K=8 **+0.5719%**,
ceiling **+0.8017%**. It dies on price, at *every* possible beam cost.

* a twin set's dRF is **the dRF of its slowest member** (48c wall = max-setter), so
  K is free and only *source eligibility* matters;
* the pool's runtime distribution is **bimodal** — 14 of 51 profiles sit far below
  the wall, the other 35 essentially *are* the wall, with **nothing at all between
  0.03% and 11.45%** weighted dRF, because M41/M42/M45 already pruned by runtime;
* restricted to affordable sources the ceiling is **+0.0956%**; a hypothetical
  **1.15×** beam (cheaper than any W=2 beam can be) reaches +0.1398% free or
  +0.2152% against a 0.30% budget ⇒ **negative NET**.

🔑 **The twin screen has a second precondition nobody had written down: the ON side
must be TIME-NEUTRAL.** Price the eligible-source set BEFORE building
(`l125_beam_price.py afford`, ~45 min of serial timing).

Also: the A/B read the beam at 2.30× under 11 parallel workers; measured
**serially it is 2.06×**. Timing under load is not timing.

### L127 — the gated-off knobs (`L127_REPORT.md`)

A 2-minute pre-screen (`l125_beam_probe.py flag`) killed `CLUSTER_BND_PERMUTE`,
`BFS_NORM`, `FREE_CLUSTER_BND`. Two survivors screened in full:

* `CLUSTER_ORD=1` — ceiling **+0.0000%**, and **verified not inert** (120/4076

## 3. 🚨 Things that will silently mislead you (this session's additions)

08-13's list still applies. These are new, and four of them cost real time here.

1. **A later stage will spend what an earlier stage bought, silently.** The first
   `refine_area` did not re-apply the boundary test that `legalise` makes at
   selection time: area_gap improved 0.955 → 0.892 while vrel went 0.1481 →
   0.1636 and the TOTAL got worse. **Nothing failed loudly** — the layout stayed
   legal and feasible, it just stopped touching the edges it was required to
   touch. When a local search optimises one term of a multiplicative cost, every
   constraint the construction enforced must be re-enforced inside the search.
2. **The boundary bit convention is `1=left, 2=right, 4=TOP, 8=BOTTOM`.** L129's
   first draft had 4/8 swapped and it was harmless only because `choose_dims` uses
   the union. Verified against `iccad2026_evaluate.py:521` AND `constructive.cpp:50`.
3. **A seed that does not respect the deployed structure gives a confident wrong
   number, ~10× off.** L128's first blend curve read 31/100 infeasible at
   blend=0.05 because it resized *clustered* members without re-packing the cluster
   interior, and `decompose()` makes each cluster one rigid unit whose intra-unit
   pairs get **no separation rows** — the overlap was welded in. (M75/M78's lesson,
   third occurrence.)
4. **Timing under parallel load is not timing.** The beam read 2.30× under 11
   workers and 2.06× serially. That flipped a "correction" into an artefact.
5. **`l124_r3_scale.py --flag NAME` needs a non-boolean value for some knobs** —
   `--on-val 2` added for `CLUSTER_ORD=2`. And `--order-sample` was added for the
   cross-sample transfer test; without it the curve picks K on the sample it scores.
6. The wrapper's `l3` is an **internal shim class** (`optimizer_constructive.py:1806`),
   not the `m53_l3_probe` module. `import m53_l3_probe as l3` populates a store the
   LP never reads.

### The next step on L129, if it is continued

**hpwl needs a genuinely better ordering, and the two cheap routes to one are
closed.** Traced: stage A's coordinates feed a bisection that keeps only the
ORDER, the order fixes the topology, and the LP then solves exact HPWL inside it.

* **IRLS reweighting is RED** (1.709 → 2.018). Wirelength alone piles every
  connected unit onto one point and `1/|delta|` makes that pull unbounded — the
  quadratic term's spread is *what makes the ordering readable*, not approximation
  error.
* **Putting HPWL in the flip search's objective is a null** (byte-identical).
  `refine_area` runs before `lp_polish`, and the LP re-derives HPWL exactly inside
  whatever topology emerges. **Pre-LP HPWL work is not a lever.**

What is left is a proper **GORDIAN alternation** — solve, partition, re-solve
constrained to the regions, partition again — which keeps wirelength optimisation
alive *while* the spreading happens rather than doing all the spreading in one
destructive step. That is a real build, not an afternoon.
