## 8. Method notes that cost time to learn

* **An instrument that loses samples silently is worse than none.** v1 of the
  profile timer printed from 51 threads to one stderr: 5100 records emitted,
  **4588 parseable**, and the loss **flipped the sign of the route A verdict**.
  Completeness is now asserted before anything prints.
* **Print a known upper bound next to the honest rows.** L203's ORACLE row
  scored *below* the shipped gate, which is impossible under separability — that
  is what exposed a units error comparing a 100-row corpus with a 240-case one.
* **Per-case wall noise on this box is ~17 %** (two identical runs, per block
  count, range 0.269–1.861). Aggregated over a band it falls to ~3 %. NET is
  dominated by ~10 heavy cases, so it inherits the per-case number. Use
  min-of-N **on both sides of a ratio**, and keep a control band that cannot
  legitimately move.
* **Do not instrument the shipping artefact.** Instruments live in
  `optimizer_l205probe.py`; the shipped tree was reverted and verified
  byte-identical when one briefly did.
* **`bash -lc` is not enough for WSL** — MSYS rewrites `/mnt/...` inside the
  quoted string and the failure is a variable expanding to *empty*, so the
  script runs and measures nothing. Use
  `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 MSYS2_ARG_CONV_EXCL='*' wsl -d Ubuntu -- bash -lc '...'`.
* **Kill background runs by their real PIDs.** A partially-killed chain ran
  concurrently with its replacement for three minutes and produced arms that
  measured a tree that no longer existed; all of them were deleted rather than
  reasoned about.
